Wave S observation review

Review the detected object, its claimed physical instance, and its map reference point. Scores are hidden. No answer is preselected. This is observation labeling—not protocol, model or campaign approval.

Instructions and decision rules
  1. Check the orange box in the full image. Use the original image to inspect any detail hidden by the outline.
  2. Category: does the visible object support the claimed class? A doorway needs opening/jamb structure. Laboratory/office entrances need readable subtype context. Colour alone is not enough.
  3. Instance: is it the particular claimed catalogue instance, not merely the right kind of object? Compare the full scene, camera viewpoint, map and numbered references. Nearest coordinates alone are not identity proof.
  4. Reference point: verify the reported map point against the claimed reference. The planar acceptance radius is ≤ 0.35 m, including equality. This is reference-point consistency, not full 3D geometry accuracy.
  5. Choose unreviewable whenever evidence is insufficient; explain why. Do not force a binary verdict. Yaw is not emitted by this new scorer, so its check is not applicable.
  6. Save a progress backup regularly. On completion, confirm your identity and download the final return. Send that JSON back without editing IDs or hashes.

Nondetections, abstentions and infrastructure failures remain in the 400-attempt ledger; they are not negative labels and are not review tasks. Correct and incorrect outcomes are not quotas for you to manufacture.
