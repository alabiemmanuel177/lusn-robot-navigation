collapsed into a signature given before their evidence exists. Final inference
and release checks may be presented together once results exist, but must preserve
their distinct hash-bound scopes. No “approve everything” checkbox is provided.

## Work that is not the human's job

The agent must finish asset construction and checks, executor/packaging code and
tests, source and environment inventories, resource checks, safe authorized
captures, completeness/coverage audits, fitting, numerical analysis, and release
packaging. Do not ask the reviewer to estimate missing empirical nuisance inputs,
repair software, create labels for absent detections, or certify machine checks.

## Why the final feedback cannot guarantee immediate closure

Coverage may still fail after the fixed attempt budget. A negative or inconclusive
scientific result is legitimate; incomplete measurements are not success. Resource
limits, invalid interventions or missing results can require more engineering or
a new prospective decision. Human feedback clears its own gates, not these facts.
The final study must report what was measured, even when the desired benefit is
not demonstrated. No all-positive outcome is required or promised.
