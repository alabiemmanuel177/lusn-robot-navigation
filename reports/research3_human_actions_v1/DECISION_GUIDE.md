## D1. Primary scientific comparison

Proposed: **B6 minus B5 on independently measured ordered-instruction completion**.
B5 uses calibrated observations with a fixed decision policy; B6 adds the
contradiction-aware policy. This comparison tests the additional decision policy,
not whether the detector is a learned vision-language model. The current provider

The other existing candidates are B6 minus B1, B2 or B4. Pick one primary contrast
and state why; selecting several requires an explicit multiple-testing design.

chair anchor. It does not establish arbitrary language understanding, realistic
