## Evidence and limits

Wave S contains99/99 jointly correct chair emissions,84/84 laboratory entrance
emissions and78/78 office entrance emissions according to the returned human
judgments. Doorways have20correct and6incorrect. These are sample-conditional
joint-correctness proportions on scheduled development views, not proof of
population precision saturation, zero future error, or per-observation p_joint=1.
Nondetections, abstentions and failures remain outside these emission denominators.

Zero observed errors admits nonzero population error. For illustration only,
under independent identical Bernoulli errors, the one-sided95% zero-error bound
solves (1-e)^n=0.05, giving e_upper=1-0.05^(1/n). The corresponding limits are
approximately2.98%,3.50%,3.77% for99,84,78 observations. These are NOT certified
bounds here: repeated views/seeds share ten worlds, class selection followed
inspection of these outcomes, and neither independent sampling nor a target
population model has been established. Pooling261 observations does not supply
a class-specific or instance-specific calibrated probability. A nontrivial
distribution-free future-error guarantee cannot be asserted from this sample.

Exact binomial method reference:
[NIST exact binomial confidence limits](https://itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman2/auxillar/exacbino.htm).
The zero-error formula above is its Bernoulli tail-inversion special case;
applicability assumptions and post-selection limitations remain explicit.

## Unresolved scientific choices

1. `p_joint approximately1.0` is not a numeric rule; a constant probability and
   detector/OCR raw-score pass-through are not equivalent. Raw matching confidence
   has not been established as joint category/instance/reference correctness.
   No arbitrary epsilon, constant or pass-through choice is silently selected.
2. P2/C/V originally require five correct and five incorrect observations per
   class plus five observations per confidence bin. The request changes the
   supervised fitting floor but supplies no exact replacement C/V admission rule.
   Constant-one predictions leave both lower bins empty. Temperature scaling of
   exact p=1 leaves it at1 (finite logit clipping is a different specified rule).
   Keeping these gates therefore does not unblock calibration certification.
3. A replacement validation estimand, independent unit, error criterion, scope
   and failure rule must be specified prospectively; they cannot be chosen after
   inspecting new validation outcomes. Original failure must remain in reporting.
4. Future C/V category/instance/reference judgments are data, not execution
   permissions. Broad authorization does not provide them. The accepted V custody
   workflow also requires the reviewer-held key and artifact-bound release record;
   neither is available from this instruction. Do not decrypt, fabricate labels,
   or silently replace the agreed human review process.
5. Navigation feasibility, power disposition, campaign gates, held-out evaluation
   and actual results remain necessary before claiming research completion.
